# Antonym-in-Context and Synonym-in-Context Questions

**Subject:** English Language Comprehension | **Section:** Vocabulary (Applied) | **Exam relevance:** MPESB along with MPPSC, Vyapam/ESB, Patwari, MP Police — this is the final and most advanced vocabulary topic in this checklist, testing whether you can correctly apply points 10-11 (Synonyms/Antonyms) to a word **as it's actually used in a sentence**, not in isolation.

---

## 1. What Makes "In-Context" Questions Different?

Straightforward synonym/antonym questions (points 10-11) test a word **in isolation** — one fixed meaning, one correct answer. **In-context questions** give a word **as used within a specific sentence**, and the correct synonym/antonym must match **that particular usage** — because many common English words carry **multiple, unrelated meanings** depending on context (the same phenomenon as Hindi's अनेकार्थी शब्द, applied here specifically to synonym/antonym selection).

---

## 2. Why Context Matters — Words With Multiple Unrelated Meanings

| Word | Meaning 1 | Meaning 2 | Meaning 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bank | financial institution | edge of a river | — |
| Novel | new/original (adjective) | a book (noun) | — |
| Fair | just/equitable | light-colored (skin/hair) | a carnival/exhibition |
| Right | correct | direction (opposite of left) | a legal entitlement |
| Sound | noise | healthy/strong ("a sound argument") | a body of water |
| Grave | serious/solemn (adjective) | a burial place (noun) | — |
| Mean | unkind (adjective) | average (statistics) | to signify (verb) |
| Light | not heavy | illumination | pale in color |
| Fast | quick | firmly fixed ("hold fast") | — |
| Fine | good quality | a monetary penalty | — |
| Present | a gift (noun) | current time (adjective) | to give/show (verb) |

Each of these words needs a **completely different synonym or antonym** depending on which sense is active in the sentence.

---

## 3. Strategy — Step by Step

1. **Read the full sentence**, not just the target word in isolation.
2. **Determine the word's part of speech as used here** (noun/verb/adjective) — this alone often eliminates most wrong senses (point 1 — Parts of speech).
3. **Determine which specific sense is active**, if multiple senses share that same part of speech.
4. **Only then select the synonym/antonym matching that specific sense** — options matching a different, unused sense of the word are deliberate distractors and must be rejected even though they're "technically" a synonym/antonym of the word in general.

---

## 4. Worked Examples

| Sentence | Active sense | Correct synonym | Correct antonym |
|---|---|---|---|
| "The bank raised interest rates." | financial institution | lending institution | — |
| "He sat on the bank of the river." | edge of a river | shore, embankment | — |
| "She gave a fair judgment." | just | impartial | unjust/biased |
| "She has fair skin." | light-colored | pale, light | dark |
| "The president's speech was grave." | serious | solemn | cheerful/light |
| "They dug a grave for the deceased." | burial place (noun) | tomb | (no adjective antonym applies — different word class) |

## 5. Practice — High-Probability Self-Test

| Sentence | Correct answer (synonym or antonym as asked) |
|---|---|
| "This is a fine piece of art." — synonym of "fine" here | excellent, exquisite |
| "He had to pay a fine for speeding." — synonym of "fine" here | penalty |
| "The mean of these numbers is 10." — meaning of "mean" here | average |
| "That was a mean thing to say." — antonym of "mean" here | kind |
| "Please hold the rope fast." — meaning of "fast" here | firmly, securely (not "quickly") |

---

## 6. Most Important Exam-Focused Points

1. **Never answer from the word alone** — the whole point of this question type is that the isolated word is ambiguous; the sentence is what disambiguates it.
2. The **part-of-speech check (Section 3, step 2)** is the fastest filter — many multi-meaning words split cleanly by whether they're used as a noun, verb, or adjective in that sentence.
3. Watch for **distractor options that are valid for a different sense of the same word** — this is the exact trap this question type is built around, and it's the main reason it's harder than plain points 10-11.
4. This topic is the natural capstone of the vocabulary section (points 10-13, 17-18, 22) — it requires all of those skills applied together in a single question, which is why it's placed last in this checklist.
