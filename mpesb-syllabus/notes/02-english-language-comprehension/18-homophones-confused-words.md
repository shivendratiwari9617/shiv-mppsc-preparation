# Homophones and Commonly Confused Words

**Subject:** English Language Comprehension | **Section:** Vocabulary | **Exam relevance:** MPESB along with MPPSC, Vyapam/ESB, Patwari, MP Police — this parallels Hindi point 15 (शब्द-युग्म); choosing the correct word between two similar-sounding options is a standard question type.

---

## 1. What are Homophones?

Words that **sound the same (or nearly the same) but differ in spelling and meaning**. "Commonly confused words" is a broader category that also includes words that merely *look* similar but aren't true homophones.

---

## 2. High-Frequency Homophones/Confused Word Pairs

| Word 1 | Meaning | Word 2 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affect | verb — to influence | Effect | noun — a result |
| Principal | noun — head of school; adj. — main | Principle | noun — a rule/belief |
| Stationary | not moving | Stationery | writing materials |
| Complement | something that completes | Compliment | praise |
| Its | possessive ("its colour") | It's | contraction of "it is/has" |
| Their | possessive | There | place; They're | contraction of "they are" |
| Your | possessive | You're | contraction of "you are" |
| Whose | possessive | Who's | contraction of "who is" |
| Accept | to receive | Except | excluding |
| Advice | noun (uncountable) | Advise | verb |
| Desert | dry land / to abandon | Dessert | sweet dish |
| Loose | not tight | Lose | to misplace/fail to win |
| Weather | climate | Whether | if (introducing a choice) |
| Break | to shatter/pause | Brake | vehicle-stopping device |
| Council | a group/committee | Counsel | advice / to advise |
| Elicit | to draw out | Illicit | illegal |
| Discreet | careful/prudent | Discrete | separate/distinct |
| Bare | naked/empty | Bear | animal / to carry, endure |
| Born | given birth | Borne | carried (past participle of "bear") |
| Cite | to quote | Sight | vision; Site | location |
| Coarse | rough texture | Course | path/subject of study |
| Peace | calm | Piece | a part |
| Right | correct/direction | Write | to compose text |
| Sole | only/fish/shoe bottom | Soul | spirit |
| Waist | body part | Waste | to squander |
| Weak | not strong | Week | 7 days |
| Hear | to listen | Here | this place |
| Flour | baking ingredient | Flower | plant bloom |
| Threw | past tense of "throw" | Through | in one side and out the other |
| Passed | past tense of "pass" | Past | time before now |

---

## 3. Strategy — Grammatical Role is Often the Key

For pairs like **affect/effect, advice/advise, principal/principle**, the fastest test is asking: **is this word functioning as a noun or a verb in the sentence?**

- Affect = **verb** ("This will affect me.")
- Effect = **noun** ("The effect was clear.") — occasionally used as a formal verb ("to effect change")
- Advice = **noun** ("Give me some advice.")
- Advise = **verb** ("Please advise me.")

## 4. Practice — High-Probability Self-Test

| Sentence | Correct word |
|---|---|
| "The weather may ___ our travel plans." | affect |
| "Follow your ___ in life." | principles |
| "Please buy some ___ for the office." | stationery |
| "She gave him a nice ___ on his work." | compliment |
| "I don't know ___ he will come." | whether |
| "He tends to ___ things when he's careless." | lose |

---

## 5. Most Important Exam-Focused Points

1. **Affect/Effect, Principal/Principle, Stationary/Stationery** are the three most repeated homophone questions across state-level exams — memorize these first.
2. Where the pair is a noun/verb split (advice/advise, effect/affect), the **grammatical-role test (Section 3)** works almost every time.
3. This topic parallels Hindi point 15 (शब्द-युग्म) — the underlying skill (distinguishing similar-sounding words by meaning and grammatical function) is the same test in both languages.
4. Contraction-based pairs (its/it's, their/there/they're, your/you're, whose/who's) are extremely common in error-spotting (point 14) as well — a misplaced apostrophe is one of the most frequently planted errors.
