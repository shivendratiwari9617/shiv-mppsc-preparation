separate yearly current-affairs update table, especially for awards, appointments, sports events,
international years, government campaigns, summits and one-line factual current events.

Current-affairs-adjacent topics outside Unit 8 are counted under their subject units, not in the strict
Unit 8 count above. Examples: MP Economic Survey data in Unit 5, recent space/science-health facts in
Unit 6, and state e-governance initiatives in Unit 9.
