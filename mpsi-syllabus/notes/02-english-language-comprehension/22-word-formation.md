# Word Formation — Prefixes and Suffixes

**Subject:** English Language Comprehension | **Section:** Vocabulary | **Exam relevance:** MPSI along with MPPSC, Vyapam/ESB, Patwari, MP Police — this parallels Hindi points 3-4 (उपसर्ग/प्रत्यय); prefix/suffix knowledge is the fastest way to decode unfamiliar words in vocabulary questions (points 10-12) and reading comprehension (point 19).

---

## 1. Prefixes vs Suffixes

| | Position | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Prefix** | Added **before** the root word | Usually changes meaning |
| **Suffix** | Added **after** the root word | Usually changes meaning **and part of speech** |

---

## 2. Negative Prefixes (Very High-Yield)

| Prefix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| un- | not | unhappy, unable |
| in- | not | incorrect, invisible |
| im- (before b/m/p) | not | impossible, immoral |
| il- (before l) | not | illegal, illogical |
| ir- (before r) | not | irregular, irresponsible |
| dis- | not/opposite | disagree, dishonest |
| non- | not | nonsense, nonexistent |
| mis- | wrongly | misunderstand, misplace |
| anti- | against | antisocial, antibiotic |

**Note:** the choice among in-/im-/il-/ir- depends on the **first letter of the root** — this is a common exam trap (e.g., "inlegal" is wrong; "illegal" is correct).

## 4. Suffixes That Form Nouns

| Suffix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -tion/-sion | act/state of | action, decision |
| -ment | result/action | agreement, development |
| -ness | quality/state | happiness, kindness |
| -ity/-ty | state/quality | equality, honesty |
| -er/-or | one who does | teacher, actor |
| -ist | one who practices | artist, scientist |
| -ship | state/condition | friendship, leadership |
| -hood | state/condition | childhood, neighborhood |
| -ance/-ence | state/quality | importance, difference |

## 5. Suffixes That Form Adjectives

| Suffix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -able/-ible | capable of | comfortable, visible |
| -ful | full of | beautiful, careful |
| -less | without | careless, hopeless |
| -ous/-ious | having the quality of | dangerous, ambitious |
| -al | relating to | natural, national |
| -ive | having a tendency | active, creative |
| -ic | relating to | historic, artistic |

## 6. Suffixes That Form Verbs and Adverbs

| Suffix | Forms | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -ize/-ise | verb (to make) | modernize, realize |
| -fy | verb (to make) | simplify, satisfy |
| -en | verb (to make) | strengthen, widen |
| -ly | adverb (in a manner of) | quickly, happily |

---

## 7. Strategy — Decoding Unfamiliar Words

1. **Identify the suffix first** to determine the part of speech — this immediately narrows down how the word functions in the sentence.
2. **Identify the prefix** to determine whether the meaning is negated, reversed, repeated, or intensified.
3. **Isolate the root word** in the middle — often a recognizable base word once the prefix/suffix is stripped away.

**Example:** "irreplaceable" = **ir-** (not) + **replace** (root) + **-able** (capable of) → "not capable of being replaced."

## 8. Practice — High-Probability Self-Test

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Correct negative prefix for "legal" | il- (illegal) |
| Correct negative prefix for "regular" | ir- (irregular) |
| Suffix that turns "happy" into a noun | -ness (happiness) |
| Suffix that turns "modern" into a verb | -ize (modernize) |
| Break down "unbelievable" into parts | un- (not) + believe (root) + -able (capable of) |

---

## 9. Most Important Exam-Focused Points

1. The **in-/im-/il-/ir- selection rule (Section 2)** based on the root's first letter is one of the most repeatedly tested prefix questions.
2. Recognizing a **suffix's part-of-speech signal (Sections 4-6)** is the fastest way to eliminate wrong answer choices in vocabulary and grammar questions alike, since it tells you what grammatical slot the word can fill.
3. This topic is the **direct decoding tool** for points 10-13 (synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms) — an unfamiliar word's prefix/suffix often gives away enough meaning to answer correctly even without prior exposure to that exact word.
4. This directly parallels Hindi points 3 (उपसर्ग) and 4 (प्रत्यय) — the same "build meaning from parts" strategy applies in both languages.
