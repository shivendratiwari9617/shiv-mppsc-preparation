# One-Word Substitution

**Subject:** English Language Comprehension | **Section:** Vocabulary | **Exam relevance:** MPSI along with MPPSC, Vyapam/ESB, Patwari, MP Police — this parallels Hindi point 14 (अनेक शब्दों के लिए एक शब्द) and is tested with equal frequency in English.

---

## 1. What is One-Word Substitution?

Replacing a **phrase or group of words** with a **single word** that conveys the same meaning precisely.

## 10. Most Important Exam-Focused Points

1. **Negative-only pairs** (words with a "cannot be..." phrase, like inevitable/incorrigible/inimitable) are extremely common — most start with a negating prefix (in-/il-/im-), tying directly to point 22 (Word formation) and point 11 (Antonyms).
2. **"-cide" and "-phobia" word families (Sections 4, 5)** are internally consistent — once you know the pattern, you can extend it to related but unlisted words.
3. Watch for **near-miss options** in MCQs — e.g., "Misanthrope" (hates mankind) vs "Philanthropist" (loves mankind) are commonly placed as confusing distractors against each other.
4. This is a pure memorization topic like Hindi point 14 — spaced repetition of this table is more effective than trying to derive answers logically.
