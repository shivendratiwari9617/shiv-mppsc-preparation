# Synonyms

**Subject:** English Language Comprehension | **Section:** Vocabulary | **Exam relevance:** MPSI along with MPPSC, Vyapam/ESB, Patwari, MP Police — "choose the synonym" is a permanent fixture of every competitive English vocabulary section.

---

## 1. What are Synonyms?

Words that have the **same or nearly the same meaning** as another word. Exact meaning may still carry a slightly different tone/intensity — competitive exams generally accept the closest-matching option.

## 3. Strategy — Using Word Formation to Guess Unfamiliar Words

If you don't recognize a word directly, **prefix/root/suffix knowledge** (see point 22 — Word formation) can help narrow down its meaning: e.g., "bene-" (good, as in **bene**volent, **bene**fit) signals a positive meaning; "mal-" (bad, as in **mal**evolent, **mal**practice) signals a negative one.

## 5. Most Important Exam-Focused Points

1. There is no shortcut to vocabulary — the words in the table above are drawn from **repeatedly tested competitive-exam vocabulary**, so treat this list as a memorization core.
2. When two options both seem plausible, pick the one matching the word's **connotation/intensity** as well as its literal meaning (e.g., "enormous" is closer to "gigantic" than to merely "big").
3. This topic pairs directly with point 11 (Antonyms) — many exams test the same word bank both ways, so learning a word's synonym and antonym together doubles your retention efficiency.
4. Root/prefix awareness (Section 3, and point 22) is the best fallback strategy for words you've never seen before.
