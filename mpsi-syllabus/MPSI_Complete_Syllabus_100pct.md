## 0. Exam pattern & marking scheme (from the official 2026 rulebook, परिशिष्ट एक/दो)

| Stage | Papers | Marks | Duration | Question type | Negative marking | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **चरण-1: प्रारंभिक परीक्षा (Prelims)** | 1 paper, 10 subjects mixed | 100 | 2 hrs | Objective (MCQ) | **None** | Purely qualifying — marks don't carry forward to final merit. Cutoff is category-wise (UR/OBC/ST/SC/EWS); ~10× the vacancy count is shortlisted for Mains. |
