# MPSI (सूबेदार/उप निरीक्षक भर्ती परीक्षा-2026) — Syllabus Coverage vs. Existing Study Material

Source syllabus: `SI_Rulebook_2026_01092026 (3).pdf`, अध्याय-3 "पाठ्यक्रम" (pages 41–43), म.प्र. कर्मचारी चयन मंडल.
Compared against: the 10 existing MPPSC unit folders documented in `SYLLABUS_COVERAGE.md` (1,743 topics /
57,045 MCQs).

**Bottom line:** MPSI shares its History/Geography/Science/Polity/Current-Affairs backbone with the MPPSC
material you already have, so those four subjects are well covered. But MPSI's exam pattern also has four
whole components that MPPSC's General Studies syllabus doesn't test at all — **Hindi & English language
comprehension, Reasoning/Analytical ability, and Numerical Aptitude** — plus a **police-specific Mains theme
(internal security, cyber-crime basics, organised crime–terrorism nexus, role of police in democracy)** that
isn't part of MPPSC GS either. Those are listed with granular topic breakdowns in the new
[`missing-topics/`](./missing-topics) folder.
